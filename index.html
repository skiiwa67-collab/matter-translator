How we work this problem

  • This is a mind exercise / thought experiment. We are trying to figure out something that, as far as we know, does not exist, and that no one is currently thinking about how to make exist.
  • Think outside the box for everything, but ground it in reality: if a solution requires something that will never happen (e.g. antimatter), say so plainly — don't pretend it's viable.
  • When generating hard questions for Eve (or anyone), don't just ask them — spin up research: pull scientific papers and prior art on the specific physics involved, then report back what was found and what might or might not be possible. That research-backed answer is the deliverable, not just the question.
  • Purpose: figure out whether this can exist and what it would take, not to ship a product.

Phase Shift Part Two

Translate a volume from A to B in under three femtoseconds. Preload the object. Flip polarity so space-time feels wrong. Resonance hits. The volumes swap because the setup made the swap inevitable.

  • No computers or copper in the timing loop.
  • The translation is inherent, like a key in a lock.
  • We swap volumes, not objects.
  • Point B must be clear. Occupied B collapses the resonance.
  • Distance does not cost extra energy.

Phase Shift Part Three

Geometry is a resonance property. Corners bunch the field; smooth surfaces spread it. A human is easier to translate than a cube if there are no sharp angles. So we stop wrapping the human and translate a bounding volume instead.

  • The capsule carries the boundary layer — one clean surface, a third of the energy, a hard containment edge.
  • The capsule is the field generator. It stays at A. The human is cargo.
  • Contact between cargo and capsule is a hard no. Suspend everything: levitate, air gap, or node placement.
  • The shape is the instruction. No switch. No processor. It just goes.

Phase Shift Part Four

Resonance lives on the shell surface. Interior volume translates A→B. Mass does not matter — only volume. Contents never touch the shell. Geometry path: cube → sphere → egg.

  • Deep-space target rejects acoustic, magnetic, free-fall, and orbit suspension shortcuts.
  • Even at L2, stray energy (sun, CMB, thermal) needs an active energy cloak around the cavity.
  • Neutrinos are untouchable — the field must be indifferent or the design fails.
  • Error budget is the design spec. Scaling is geometry-driven, not mass-driven.
  • Framing: thinking test, not a product, nobody being translated.
